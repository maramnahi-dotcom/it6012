Four ways to stay safe

Small habits can make a big difference when you use websites and apps.

🔑

Strong passwords

Use long, different passwords for your accounts. Do not share them.

📩

Scam messages

Be careful with urgent messages asking for money or account details.

🔐

Extra verification

Turn on two-factor authentication when it is available.

🔗

Suspicious links

Check the website address and avoid links from unexpected messages.

What does a scam look like?

These examples show warning signs you should notice.

EXAMPLE 1

Fake bank warning

Your account will be blocked in 10 minutes! Click here and enter your password now.

Warning sign: The message creates panic and asks you to enter your password through a link.

EXAMPLE 2

Fake prize

Congratulations! You won a free phone. Pay a small delivery fee to claim it.

Warning sign: An unexpected prize asks you to pay money before receiving anything.
