Website Planning & Design

This page explains the planning, design choices, wireframes, and mockups for the TechEase website.

Project Planning

Project purpose

TechEase helps older adults in Bahrain learn to use smartphones, online services, and the internet safely and confidently.

Target audience

Older adults who want clear, beginner-friendly technology instructions and a safe way to practise.

Website objectives

  • Explain common technology tasks step by step.
  • Provide practice activities and simulations.
  • Help users recognize online risks.
  • Make the pages easy to read and navigate.

Website structure

  1. Home
  2. Smartphone Basics
  3. Online Services
  4. Stay Safe Online
  5. Planning & Design
  6. Reflection & AI
  7. Overview

User journey

  1. Visit the Home page.
  2. Choose a learning topic.
  3. Follow the step-by-step guide.
  4. Practise using an activity or simulation.
  5. Review the safety tips and next steps.

Website requirements

  • Navigation links connect the seven website pages.
  • Learning pages provide clear instructions and activities.
  • The registration form checks the entered information.
  • Pages adapt to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

Design Decisions

Clear and simple layout

Pages use familiar navigation and organized sections to help users find information without confusion.

Step-by-step learning

Instructions are divided into small steps, with practice activities to help users learn at their own pace.

Senior-friendly interface

Large readable text, clear labels, visible buttons, and uncluttered pages support easier use.

Consistent design

The same colours, typography, navigation, and card styles are used throughout the website.

Typography Selection

Poppins was selected for its clear, simple letter shapes. The headings use a bold weight to make sections easy to identify, while regular body text supports comfortable reading.
