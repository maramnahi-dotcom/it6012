Project Overview & Documentation

Project Details

Website Title: TechEase (Technology Made Easy for Seniors)

Category: Educational & Institutional Support Website

Target Audience: In Bahrain, adult learners and elderly citizens (60 years of age and older) are looking for secure, self-paced digital literacy.

Problem Statement: Some older adults find smartphones and online services difficult to use and worry about mistakes or scams.

Project Objectives: Provide zero-risk interactive simulations, increase digital independence, and educate against fraud.

Requirement Analysis

Functional Requirements

  • Navigation linking exactly seven pages.
  • Registration form on the Home page.
  • First and last names: at least 3 characters each.
  • Mobile number: exactly 8 digits.
  • Valid email format and success/error messages.
  • Learning guides and interactive simulations.
  • Hints, restart options, and text-size controls.

Non-Functional Requirements

  • Performance: Optimised images and code.
  • Accessibility: Readable text and labelled controls.
  • Security: No personal data stored by the demo.
  • Usability: Simple instructions and navigation.
  • Responsiveness: Desktop, tablet, and mobile support.

UI Style Guide

Primary Colour: Deep teal-blue — #086080

Secondary Colour: Light blue — #D5EFFB

Accent Colour: Bright blue — #0BA9ED

Heading Font: Poppins Bold

Body Font: Poppins Regular, with Arial and sans-serif as fallbacks

Components

  • Buttons with clear labels and rounded corners.
  • Cards with consistent spacing and white backgrounds.
  • Navigation with an active-page indicator.
  • Forms with visible labels and validation messages.

Design Decisions

Clear headings, short instructions, and large controls help beginners understand and use the website.

Responsive Design Plan

  • Desktop: Multiple content columns.
  • Tablet: Fewer columns and wrapping navigation.
  • Mobile: Single-column layout and full-width inputs.

AI Utilization Plan

  • ChatGPT: Content, code, and debugging assistance.
  • Canva AI — planned: Visual design and mockups.
  • Gemini — planned: Content and usability review.

Expected outcomes include clearer content, reviewed code, and improved designs. Actual use of at least three AI tools must be documented.
